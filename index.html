The vision

Keep the part that gets thrown away.

Almost everything written about working with AI arrives as a finished result with the learning taken out. The result is the least useful part of it.

So we keep the rest, and we publish it as we go — starting with this page's own drafts, below.

What that commits us to

Three rules

  1. 01
    Show the working-out.

    The notes, the dead ends, and the draft that did not work, alongside whatever finally did.

  2. 02
    Claim nothing unshipped.

    Nothing here describes work that does not exist yet. That is why this page is short.

  3. 03
    Correct it in the open.

    When we get something wrong, the correction lands here too, and the history keeps both.
